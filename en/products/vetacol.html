Why VETACOL

Immediately after birth:
The Survival Golden Hour

Newborn calves rely entirely on maternal antibodies ingested during the first hours of life. When colostrum intake or quality is suboptimal, calves become highly susceptible to scours and respiratory failure. VETACOL bridges this critical gap, ensuring high early survival and uniform growth.

  1. COL
    Colostrum + Whey Dual Immunoglobulins

    Bioactive maternal colostrum and whey immunoglobulins accelerate the establishment of neonatal defense barriers.

  2. BIO
    Bacillus subtilis Probiotic Shield

    Beneficial spore-forming bacteria populate the intestinal tract, mitigating pathogen adhesion and digestive stress.

  3. MCT
    Rapid MCT + Multi-Vitamin Energy

    Readily absorbable medium-chain triglycerides and vital vitamins support hypothermic and weak newborns immediately.

  4. SYR
    Pre-measured 15 ml Single Syringe

    No mixing or measuring needed. Direct oral delivery ensures exact dosage and stress-free barn application.
