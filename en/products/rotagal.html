Why ROTAGAL

The root cause of breeding farm loss:
Manage calf diarrhea with a single vaccine

Diarrhea and dehydration are leading causes of newborn mortality and stunted growth. Administered to pregnant cows prior to calving, ROTAGAL significantly boosts colostral antibodies, forming an impenetrable protective barrier during the critical early days of life.

  1. 3in1
    Tri-Pathogen Simultaneous Protection

    Bovine Rotavirus, Coronavirus, and enterotoxigenic E. coli (K99) — the three major etiologic agents of calf scours covered under a single formulation.

  2. SHOT
    Single One-Shot Pre-Calving Injection

    Formulated with Montanide ISA 206 mineral-oil adjuvant ensuring sustained antibody titers and maximal maternal transfer through colostrum.

  3. GMP
    EU GMP Direct Import · Globally Proven

    Manufactured by Pharmagal (Slovakia) and exported to over 40 nations including Germany, the UK, and Australia; officially supplied in Korea by Korea Agro.
