Clinical Mechanism

Immediate Mucosal Barrier Coating
& Enterotoxin Adsorption 5-Complex

MONSMECTA is strictly supplied through verified veterinary channels rather than general commercial retail. Formulated for viral enteritis (parvovirus, rotavirus, coronavirus), bacterial diarrhea, and acute digestive collapse to physically coat damaged villi and expedite stool normalization.

  1. NANO
    Ultra-Fine Nano Montmorillonite

    High specific surface area phyllosilicate mineral bonds with the intestinal mucus layer, adsorbing pathogenic enterotoxins and mycotoxins for excretion.

  2. PAT
    Patented Bacillus subtilis MORI (Patent No. 201180042602.8)

    Contains natural 1-deoxynojirimycin (DNJ), suppressing enteric pathogens and restoring microfloral homeostasis rapidly.

  3. CELL
    Epithelial Regeneration Vitamin A & Dextrose

    Accelerates cellular restoration of desquamated epithelial lining and provides immediately accessible metabolic energy against dehydration collapse.

  4. ION
    Electrolyte Balance (Sodium Acetate & Propionate)

    Quickly counters acidosis and electrolyte drainage caused by severe diarrhea, stabilizing systemic osmolarity.

Mode of Action

MONSMECTA 3-Phase Defense Mechanism

From toxin trapping to epithelial repair and microbiome stabilization.

STEP 1. Physical Mucosal Shield

Layered silicate nanostructures coat the gastrointestinal lining, shielding inflamed tissue from gastric acid, bile salts, and direct viral invasion.

STEP 2. Enterotoxin & Viral Adsorption

Captures bacterial endotoxins, rota/corona viral capsids, and toxic amine byproducts within intercellular lamellae for safe expulsion.

STEP 3. Villi Repair & Flora Restoration

Vitamin A and vital electrolytes speed up villi regeneration while probiotic Bacillus colonies colonize and dominate the intestinal lumen.
