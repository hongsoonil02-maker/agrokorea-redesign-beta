Why PARVOGEL

Universal Neonatal Care
Across All Species

Neonatal diarrhea is the most frequent clinical challenge confronting farmers and veterinary practitioners across all livestock sectors. PARVOGEL unites mineral adsorption science with probiotics streamlining herd management into one proven product.

  1. NANO
    Ultra-Fine Nano Montmorillonite

    Exceptional specific surface area physically adsorbs toxins excess luminal water and protects gut mucosa.

  2. BIO
    Bacillus subtilis Microflora Support

    Active beneficial microbes restore gut microbiome equilibrium and suppress pathogenic proliferation.

  3. ALL
    One Solution for All Newborn Livestock

    Universal compatibility across cattle swine sheep goats and horses simplifies inventory management.
