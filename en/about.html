CEO Message

Leaping Forward as a
Total Bio Solution Provider

Integrity & HonestyMutual RespectPassion for Results

Welcome to Korea Agro Co. Ltd. Dedicated to realizing sustainable bio-agriculture we specialize in supplying essential solutions across feed raw materials functional additives livestock environmental improvers and veterinary medicines.

Anchored in our core values of integrity mutual respect and dedication to measurable results we have steadily grown through deep trust with our farm clients and global partners. Our proprietary IAMC mineral complex patented StarKing probiotics and exclusive partnerships with global pioneers such as VTR (digestive enzymes) and DDC (flavor enhancers) stand testament to our innovation and global reach.

Expanding into premium calf vaccines including ROTAGAL® from 2024 we are solidifying our position as an all-encompassing Total Bio Solution Provider. Through continuous R&D and ESG-centered management we pledge to enrich the lives of farmers animals and our society.

CEO Hyo-sun Hong

ESG Commitment

Social Cooperative 'Resources & Circulation'
Practicing Sustainable Bio-Circular Economy

Korea Agro actively spearheads environmental social and governance (ESG) stewardship through innovative green technology. Utilizing our FECOWA (Fermented Coffee Waste) patented technology we developed 'StarKing' a groundbreaking fermented probiotic made from spent coffee grounds pioneering a circular resource model that reduces agricultural waste. Through formal partnership with the 'Resources & Circulation' cooperative we practice shared value creation and eco-friendly commercialization.
